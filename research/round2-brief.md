# Round 2 State-Machine Brief

This brief deliberately removes marketing names from the eight quarterfinalists.
Jurors must judge the actual collaboration loop and may not award novelty points
for preview, approval, dynamic registration, undo or receipts alone.

## Q1 — performance-caption constraint repair (CueKind)

- **Audience/pain:** small theatres, schools and live-event teams whose caption
  files may be textually correct yet unreadable or artistically destructive.
- **Human-only input:** lock exact punchline, laugh, silence and entrance beats;
  choose between equally legal timing/placement alternatives.
- **Agent computation:** exhaustively audit cue intervals and search bounded
  timing, speaker-label and screen-position changes under a declared profile.
- **Visible transition:** seven red timeline conflicts slide to zero while every
  locked cue remains fixed; before/after playback stays on the same stage.
- **Falsifiable proof:** max characters per second, overlaps, ambiguous speaker
  turns, protected-beat changes and position conflicts are recalculated from
  the exported cue track.
- **Artifact:** editable WebVTT plus a constraint certificate bound to its digest.
- **Closest field:** wcrew/RelayLab/Redini-style constraint/change-set flows;
  no inspected product owns performance-caption timing as its engine.
- **Fatal risk:** timeline polish and rule credibility. Use one declared,
  configurable production profile and never claim universal accessibility
  certification.

## Q2 — bird-visible facade pattern planning sandbox (Flightpath)

- **Audience/pain:** architects and community building teams comparing visible
  facade marking patterns before specialist review.
- **Human-only input:** protect view/aesthetic zones and choose among Pareto
  alternatives; do not allow the agent to declare a building safe.
- **Agent computation:** enumerate a bounded facade grid against identical seeded
  illustrative trajectories and a disclosed spacing/coverage surrogate.
- **Visible transition:** repeated paths that strike the baseline window deflect
  around an optimized pattern using about seven percent marked coverage.
- **Falsifiable proof:** identical seeds, explicit collision geometry, protected
  zone preservation, exact marked area and complete candidate frontier.
- **Artifact:** SVG pattern/design sheet plus a simulator certificate.
- **Closest field:** Hopyard and Chisel visual planning; unrelated naming collision
  with FlightSweeper. Rename to Featherline/SafePane if selected.
- **Fatal risk:** arbitrary or misleading science. Present it as an inspectable
  planning sandbox and surrogate comparison, never prediction or certification.

## Q3 — duplex zine imposition compiler (ZineFold)

- **Audience/pain:** artists and community publishers turning an intended story
  order into a correctly imposed eight-page duplex sheet.
- **Human-only input:** lock narrative order, spreads, blanks and binding direction.
- **Agent computation:** solve the finite page/signature arrangement or return a
  smallest unsatisfiable constraint set.
- **Visible transition:** an impossible arrangement is rejected, then a legal
  sheet folds in animation into the exact intended reading order.
- **Falsifiable proof:** every imposed cell maps to one page and the deterministic
  fold simulation reconstructs pages 1–8 without reversal.
- **Artifact:** printable SVG/PDF-ready layout and imposition certificate.
- **Closest field:** general creative editors, but no inspected imposition engine.
- **Fatal risk:** one fixed formula may not need an agent and one mirrored page
  destroys credibility.

## Q4 — live DOM focus-intent repair (Focus Treaty)

- **Audience/pain:** frontend teams debugging a keyboard trap and broken focus
  restoration in an actual modal.
- **Human-only input:** record the intended keyboard journey and confirm which
  element should regain focus.
- **Agent computation:** compare actual focus-event traces, isolate a minimal
  failing transition and stage a bounded DOM/class/handler repair.
- **Visible transition:** the same keyboard replay changes from trapped/red to a
  complete green path against real focusable elements.
- **Falsifiable proof:** ordered focus events, active-element assertions and exact
  restore target; no claim of general WCAG certification.
- **Artifact:** trace, patch diff and replay certificate.
- **Closest field:** InclusivePatch, Accessible Canvas and other accessibility
  remediation/testing entries.
- **Fatal risk:** if the trace or replay is canned, the entire claim collapses;
  if real, the visual/product ceiling may still be modest.

## Q5 — uncertainty-aware ceramic reconstruction (ShardWise)

- **Audience/pain:** conservation teams exploring uncertain joins and treatment
  sequences without allowing an optimization score to choose conservation ethics.
- **Human-only input:** select treatment philosophy and reject a physically or
  historically implausible join based on curator knowledge.
- **Agent computation:** rank bounded geometric joins with exposed evidence and
  uncertainty, then search a valid assembly sequence.
- **Visible transition:** a scattered sherd graph becomes a reconstructed vessel;
  the human choice changes invisible seams to deliberate gold seams.
- **Falsifiable proof:** all chosen joins cite fit features/confidence and the
  assembly sequence never depends on a piece before it is supported.
- **Artifact:** reconstruction plan, provenance and editable SVG/scene.
- **Closest field:** Chisel/Hopyard visual geometry.
- **Fatal risk:** bespoke geometry and visual work; a hidden arbitrary fit score
  makes it look like a reskinned jigsaw or fake conservation authority.

## Q6 — consent-revocable photo-set composer (ConsentCut Media)

- **Audience/pain:** small editorial teams building a purpose-specific image set
  whose subject permissions may change after an initial layout.
- **Human-only input:** declare editorial purpose, lock composition/story choices,
  and supply/revoke each synthetic subject's consent.
- **Agent computation:** search a pre-tagged asset graph for compatible substitutes
  while preserving manual pins.
- **Visible transition:** one revocation instantly invalidates two tiles and the
  agent recomposes only the unlocked positions.
- **Falsifiable proof:** every used asset has a purpose-compatible consent edge;
  withdrawn subjects are absent and pinned tiles retain coordinates.
- **Artifact:** editable collage plus consent/provenance manifest.
- **Closest field:** Design Studio, Redini, RelayLab, TimeTrail and other visual
  editors; consent revocation is the surviving mechanism.
- **Fatal risk:** the editor category is extremely crowded and attractive
  rights-safe assets consume build/film time. Never imply face recognition.

## Q7 — performer-stated stage-path solver (ChoreoGraph)

- **Audience/pain:** small choreography teams checking discrete stage routes
  against constraints that performers themselves declare.
- **Human-only input:** lock motifs/soloist/entrance beats; each performer enters
  their own movement constraints. No ability or body inference is permitted.
- **Agent computation:** search bounded space-time paths for three performers and
  explain any smallest unresolved collision set.
- **Visible transition:** three animated collisions disappear while locked motifs
  remain unchanged.
- **Falsifiable proof:** no two occupied stage cells overlap at a time step; all
  stated constraints and locked beats remain true.
- **Artifact:** editable route/cue plan and replay certificate.
- **Closest field:** Hopyard/wcrew/Duet plus many timeline tools.
- **Fatal risk:** ethical framing and a simplified movement model are easy to
  attack; animation polish is substantial.

## Q8 — stratigraphic inconsistency explorer (Field Evidence Time Machine)

- **Audience/pain:** field-school learners and archaeologists checking whether
  observations can coexist across stratigraphic precedence relationships.
- **Human-only input:** move/record one observed artifact layer as physical ground
  truth and decide which competing interpretation remains plausible.
- **Agent computation:** maintain a partial order, find cycles/inconsistencies and
  produce a smallest conflicting evidence set without erasing dissent.
- **Visible transition:** a layered section animates into three highlighted
  paradoxes when the observation changes; alternative chronologies recompute.
- **Falsifiable proof:** each chronology is a topological order of accepted edges;
  each rejection carries a minimal cycle witness.
- **Artifact:** evidence graph, alternative chronologies and replayable field note.
- **Closest field:** Particle Atlas and Interactive Star Lab.
- **Fatal risk:** weaker immediate market impact and three-minute comprehension;
  a generic notebook/graph presentation loses its novelty.

## Juror output required

For each Q1–Q8:

1. Give the strongest affirmative winning case in no more than four sentences.
2. Give the fatal attack a skeptical judge would make.
3. Score WebMCP Leverage, Execution, Impact and Creativity out of 25.
4. Estimate a zero-dependency static MVP in hours, including native testing,
   deployment, recording and submission buffer.
5. Select exactly four semifinalists and identify one conditional pivot trigger.

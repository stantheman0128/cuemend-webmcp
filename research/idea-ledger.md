# WebMCP Challenge — 100-Idea Ledger

Generated from ten independent category passes. Every candidate assumes a browser-visible shared state, typed atomic tools, deterministic validation, preview before consequential actions, human confirmation, and a resettable synthetic demo unless stated otherwise. The target is one polished vertical slice deliverable before the live 2026-09-04 08:00 UTC deadline.

Scoring frame: Stage 1 must show real, non-trivial WebMCP. Stage 2 scores WebMCP Leverage, Execution, Potential Impact, and Creativity/Ambition equally; ties break in that order.

The implementation scan changes the novelty baseline. “Agent prepares, human
approves,” an activity log, a receipt, undo, dynamic tool counts, or a large tool
catalog cannot carry a submission by themselves. The rarer opportunities are:

- a fine-grained human edit that invalidates part of an agent plan and visibly
  forces a new computation;
- visual perception and semantic tools that complement rather than duplicate
  each other;
- a WebMCP-only bounded capability that creates a useful collaborative move the
  ordinary UI cannot efficiently express;
- real public data or a truthful deterministic fixture with exact provenance;
- an authority boundary that is itself visible and central to the experience.

## Saturated versus open territory

Highly saturated:

- Generic shopping, comparison, cart, payment, or cancellation.
- Booking, meeting, workforce scheduling, and HR.
- Generic dashboard, form, CRM, CRUD, inbox, task, or all-in-one workspace.
- General coding workspace, dependency map, canvas, WebMCP adapter/registry, or security scanner.
- A normal product with only an approval modal and activity log added.
- Games without a compelling new human/agent division of labor.

Stronger remaining shape:

1. A specific, defensible domain engine.
2. Agent and human share one visible live state.
3. The flow contains a real conflict, stale state, or adversarial branch.
4. The human makes an irreducibly subjective/ethical/artistic or physical-ground-truth decision.
5. The agent supplies structured search, simulation, optimization, or exhaustive checking.
6. The result is proved with a replay, certificate, receipt, measurable invariant, or exported artifact.
7. Tools appear/retire when authority or state actually changes.
8. The produced work remains in the app for continued human editing.

## Video direction to beat the field

- Open on the signature failure/result inside 10 seconds. No logo reel, sign-up, setup, or typing preamble.
- Keep every 10–15 seconds visually active: tool call, state change, conflict, human decision, verification.
- Show one excellent complete path rather than a tour of features.
- Show the ChatGPT/in-app agent actually calling the tools alongside readable product UI.
- Include one blocked/stale/adversarial branch, one human-only decision, and one objective final proof.
- Use a direct screen capture at readable scale. Avoid oblique camera shots, reflective screens, tiny logs, waiting, or narration unsupported by the image.
- Target roughly 2:00–2:30 to preserve margin under the strict under-three-minute requirement.

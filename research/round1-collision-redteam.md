# Round 1 — Adversarial Novelty and Collision Cut

Evidence cutoff: 2026-09-04. I reviewed all 100 entries in `idea-ledger.md` against the inspected competitor/video set in `competitive-landscape.md`.

Collision scale:

- **0** — no material mechanism collision found.
- **1** — only a domain, visual, or generic workflow resemblance.
- **2** — one important shared mechanic; differentiation is still legible in ten seconds.
- **3** — a judge will make a direct comparison; the surviving mechanism must be proved, not described.
- **4** — near-duplicate; needs a major mechanism pivot.
- **5** — direct collision; remove from the tournament.

The cut is mechanism-first. “Human approval,” an activity trail, undo, preview, and dynamic tool counts are table stakes after Redini Atelier, Reviewline, LAST DOOR, Hopyard Designer, and Ninth Tool; they do not count as novelty by themselves.

## Ranked top four

1. **Flightpath** — best combined novelty, ten-second visual payoff, credible public impact, deterministic optimization, and buildability. It also avoids the most saturated categories while still looking like a complete product.
2. **ShardWise** — most original human/agent authority split and a beautiful artifact; slightly lower because fit/science credibility is easier for a judge to attack.
3. **CueKind** — strongest professional workflow and exportable result; lower novelty because wcrew, Redini, and RelayLab make constraint-solving plus editable preview table stakes.
4. **Field Evidence Time Machine** — clearest reasoning-as-animation concept; slightly weaker immediate market impact than the first three.

## Round-2 instruction

Strip the names and domains from the top four and judge only the state machine. If a proposal cannot specify (1) the human-only input, (2) the agent-only computation, (3) the falsifiable invariant, (4) the visible shared-state transition, and (5) the durable artifact in one sentence each, eliminate it before implementation.
